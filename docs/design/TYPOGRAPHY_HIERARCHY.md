# Typography Hierarchy

- **Headings**: Playfair Display (Serif luxury aesthetic)
  - H1: `text-4xl` to `text-6xl` font-serif font-bold tracking-tight
  - H2: `text-2xl` to `text-3xl` font-serif font-bold
  - H3: `text-xl` font-serif font-medium
- **Body & UI**: Inter (Clean modern sans-serif)
  - Body: `text-sm` text-salon-muted leading-relaxed
  - Labels: `text-xs` uppercase tracking-widest
